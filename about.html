Who we are & what drives us

A multidisciplinary community of researchers delivering open, rigorous, and policy-relevant scholarship at the forefront of financial innovation.

Our mission

Digital assets, artificial intelligence, and frontier technologies are no longer at the margins of finance- they are fundamental forces reshaping money, markets, and monetary policy. Yet the field remains flooded with hype, leaving a critical gap for evidence-based insight. Our mission is to produce credible, replicable research that bridges frontier theory and market reality- equipping academics, regulators, and industry leaders to navigate and shape the future of digital finance.

Three commitments:
  1. Open science: we publish code and data wherever possible.
  2. Methodological rigour: careful identification, robust inference, and honest uncertainty.
  3. Engagement: bridging academia, industry, and public policy.

Associate Professor Cuong Nguyen leads the New Zealand Research Cluster on Financial Innovation & Digital Assets and serves as Editor of The Journal of FinTech and Digital Assets. His work spans financial econometrics, digital finance, financial technology, and the economics of digital asset markets.
