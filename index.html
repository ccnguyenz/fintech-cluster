About the community

The Research Cluster on Financial Innovation & Digital Assets stands at the forefront of the modern financial revolution. We brings together world-class economists, finance scholars, data scientists, and legal researchers to define the future of money, markets, and intelligent systems.

Modern finance is undergoing a monumental transformation—driven by the rapid digitisation of assets and the rise of artificial intelligence. Our cluster pioneers the research required to shape this new paradigm, bridging frontier theory and market reality by combining rigorous empirical methods with deep engagement across industry, regulatory bodies, and the open-source ecosystem. Our agenda addresses the sector's most critical frontiers: from crypto-asset pricing, decentralized exchange microstructure, and real-world asset tokenisation, to the welfare implications of central bank digital currencies, AI-driven market dynamics, and evolving policy landscapes. We believe true innovation demands accountability: our work is strictly open, replicable, and grounded in real-world market data.
